Soot & Summer privacy policy

Last updated: 28 September 2026

Soot & Summer puts little cats on your phone's screen, on top of your other apps. It has no accounts, no ads and no analytics.

What stays on your phone

Your settings (which cats are out, their names, looks and hats, the size, and the app you picked for a double-tap) are saved only on your phone. Android may include them in your own phone backup to your Google account if you have backup turned on. We (the developer) never receive them.

Cats from your photos

If you make a cat from a photo, you pick one photo with Android's photo picker, and the app can see only that photo. It reads it while you tap it, on your phone, and keeps just the colours you picked. The photo itself is never saved, copied or sent anywhere.

Permissions

  • Display over other apps: so the cats can walk on top of the apps you use. The cats only see where your finger touches them; they don't read what's on your screen.
  • Notifications and a foreground service: Android requires a notification while the cats are on screen. It also has buttons to call them, send them home or put them away.
  • List of installed apps: only so you can pick an app that opens when you double-tap a cat. The list never leaves your phone.
  • Internet: see below.

What leaves your phone, and why

  • Launch-week check. During launch week (late October 2026) the app asks a Google server for today's date, to see whether you get the launch-week look. Nothing about you or your phone is sent; like any web server, Google's sees your IP address. See Google's privacy policy.
  • Cat Pass purchase. Buying the pass is handled entirely by Google Play. We never see your payment details. See Google's privacy policy.

We do not collect, store, sell or share any personal information.

Children

Soot & Summer collects no personal information from anyone, including children.

Deleting your data

Uninstall the app and everything it saved is gone.
